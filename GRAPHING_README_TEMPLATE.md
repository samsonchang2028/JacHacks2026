# Quorum

> An objection only matters if it is countable, on the record, and aimed at whoever actually decides.

## What Quorum does

Quorum turns a local civic concern into:

1. a sourced issue graph,
2. an impact-community map,
3. a decision and procedural path,
4. potentially relevant legal and evidentiary connections,
5. precedent-backed recourse options,
6. a targeted outreach package.

## Demo cases

- Portsmouth Square, San Francisco
- Great Highway / Proposition K, San Francisco

## Core insight

The impact zone and the decision zone are often different.

Quorum uses Jac walkers to traverse seven graph layers and identify that mismatch.

## Seven layers

1. Issue and signal
2. Narrative and evidence
3. Community and stakeholder
4. Institution and decision
5. Law and procedure
6. Precedent and action
7. Campaign and activation

## Why Jac

The answer is relational and multi-hop. Jac nodes and typed edges model the civic system; walkers move through the issue, community, decision, legal, precedent, and campaign layers.

## Safety and limitations

- Informational only; not legal advice.
- Procedural facts are sourced or shown as gaps.
- Evidence-to-law connections are potential relevance, not legal conclusions.
- Proximity does not imply stakeholder support.
- Paid-media output is dry-run unless explicitly stated otherwise.
- No sensitive-trait targeting.

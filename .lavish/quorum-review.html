Recommended build order

The smallest sequence that can still land the demo

This ordering prefers demo reliability over abstract completeness. If the sequence feels wrong, annotate the slice you would reorder.

Slice 1 Fixture-first graph skeleton

Create the node and edge types, then load just enough cached Portsmouth Square and Prop K data to drive the walkers offline.

Slice 2 Decision and impact traversal

Implement DecisionWalker, ImpactWalker, and DivergenceCheck before spending time on richer AI outputs.

Slice 3 Procedural playbook with citations

Put verified source state in the UI early so nothing later encourages guessing deadlines or channels.

Slice 4 Evidence and precedent ranking

Once the graph and cited facts exist, add ranked narrative and precedent outputs to deepen the Portsmouth Square walkthrough.

Slice 5 Outreach packet generation

Draft English and Chinese outreach only after the procedural payload is locked and visibly verified.

Guardrail Do not hardcode the punchline

The divergence warning, deadlines, and channels should show up because the walkers found them, not because the UI knows the case.

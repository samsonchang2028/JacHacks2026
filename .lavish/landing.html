Caring is not the missing ingredient.

Civic engagement fails in three ways, and they need opposite fixes.

Heard too little

It never becomes countable

People hold a position and tell each other. It never reaches the channel that counts, before the deadline.

Heard too much

Volume arrives, standing does not

Hundreds of near-identical letters get recognised as bulk and weighed as one. The countable unit is a distinct submitter.

Heard by the wrong people

Aimed at the wrong electorate

Well-funded organising, pointed at the people already affected — who already agree — not at whoever decides.
